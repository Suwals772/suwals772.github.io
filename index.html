🌸 BloomFX - Grow Digital Flowers With Your Hands

Download BloomFX for Windows

BloomFX creates digital flowers in real-time. The software uses your webcam to track your hand movements. It then draws 3D flowers using these movements as a guide. This process relies on computer vision to turn physical gestures into procedural art.

📋 System Requirements

Before you run BloomFX, ensure your computer meets these standards:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11
  • Processor: Intel Core i5 or equivalent
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: Integrated or dedicated webcam with at least 720p resolution
  • Storage: 200 MB of free disk space

📥 How to Install

Follow these steps to set up the software on your machine:

  1. Visit the official download page.
  2. Look for the section labeled Assets.
  3. Select the file ending in .exe.
  4. Save the file to your computer.
  5. Double-click the downloaded file to start the installation.
  6. Follow the prompts on your screen to complete the setup process.

✨ How to Use BloomFX

Once you install the program, launch it from your desktop icon. The application will trigger your webcam automatically. Place your hand in front of the camera. The software identifies your palm and fingers. As you move your hand, the application generates stems and petals based on your path.

If the program fails to see your hand, check these items:

  • Ensure your room has good lighting.
  • Keep your hand within the frame of the video.
  • Close other programs that might use your webcam.

⚙️ Features

BloomFX provides a unique way to interact with your computer. The core features include:

  • Real-time hand tracking that works without external sensors.
  • Procedural generation that ensures every flower looks different.
  • Fluid animation style based on the path of your movement.
  • Support for high-definition webcam inputs.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues, try these common fixes:

The program does not open: Restart your computer. Verify that you have administrative rights to run applications.

The video feed is black: Check your privacy settings in Windows. Ensure that your camera has permission to share video with BloomFX.

Performance is slow: Lower the resolution of your camera settings if possible. Close background apps to free up your computer processor.
